VERTEX EDITOR · QUICK GUIDE

Anchor: resize around one vertex

Anchor holds one vertex still while the others resize around it.

1. Select and set Anchor

Select three vertices (Shift-click to add). Press Z, then hold 1 while dragging a box around the vertex to keep fixed.

2. Resize

Drag in empty viewport space. The green Anchor stays in place.

VERTEX EDITOR · QUICK GUIDE

Align: line up three spike tips

All three bases overlap in Front view. Only the forward and rear tips lean left and right. Align them to the straight middle tip, then rotate to see three equal, upright spikes.

1. Select the tips and set Align

In Front view, select the three tips. Press Z, then Shift+1. Drag a box around the middle tip.

2. Align from the front

Pull until Front view shows one complete spike.

3. Inspect from another view

Rotate upward and sideways. The three separate spikes are the same size and point the same way; their tips lined up according to the Front viewpoint.

UV WRAPPER · QUICK GUIDE

Live Select: manipulate UV coordinates live

Live Select lets you select one or more vertices in the live 3D preview while working in the UV map, then manipulate their UV coordinates immediately. Use it to separate overlapping coordinates.

1. Select vertices in the live preview

Hold A and drag over visible vertices in the live preview. Their matching UV coordinates become selected.

2. Separate overlapping coordinates

Select another visible set with A + click. Press Ctrl+E to include connected vertices, then M for Move. Drag its UV coordinates away from the overlap. Repeat for other visible sets.

AUTO CORPSE · QUICK GUIDE

Auto Corpse: adjust the fit

Use the body as a reference, then adjust any parts that need a closer fit.

1. Compare the fit

Keep Body outline on. Toggle Show body to compare the generated corpse with the body, then turn it off to see the parts clearly.

2. Move, stretch and widen parts

Double-click a part. Drag the center handle to move it, an end handle to stretch it, and the side handle to adjust its width. Use the outline to judge the fit; repeat wherever needed.

3. Set height and tint

Use Height to lower or raise the bones; this example uses 80%. Set Color Tint to R 225 · G 255 · B 100 for the result shown. Check Show body again, then click Create when the fit is right. Auto Corpse also sets up Decay Flesh and Decay Bone, including body and corpse visibility.

Glow Up: add hero glow

Add hero glow, adjust its ground size, and inspect the result.

1. Open Glow Up

From the main screen, click EMTR, then Glow Up.

2. Adjust the ground glow

Pause the preview. Drag the ground-glow edge a little to adjust its size.

3. Create and inspect

Click Create and close EMTR. Open Animations, select Stand and press Play. Alt-drag to inspect the glows during playback.
